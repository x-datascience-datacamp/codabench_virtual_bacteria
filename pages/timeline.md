# Timeline

The competition runs as a **single phase**. The leaderboard's public columns
give you live feedback on a new-gene × new-condition split of known species,
while the hidden private columns (held-out species, near and far) decide the
final ranking. Up to **5 submissions per day**.

- **Start:** 2026-01-05
- **End:** 2026-03-31

The final ranking is taken from the private (hidden) NB-NLL at the close of
the phase. Because the public and private splits differ, a model that only
chases the public columns will not rank well — aim for generalization to
unseen species.
