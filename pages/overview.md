How strongly is a bacterial gene expressed under given conditions? From the
genome around the gene, its annotation and the experimental conditions of an
RNA-seq run, predict the read counts of the gene in that run, across many
bacterial species. The data was curated at the Institut Pasteur.

The **Participation** page describes the data, the metric and how to submit,
and the **Timeline** page gives the dates.
